By topic

Dimensionless numbers and friction factors

  • Reynolds, Prandtl
  • Nusselt, Nusselt_annulus
  • friction_factor_Colebrook_White, friction_factor_Tkachenko_Mileikovskyi

Thermal resistances

  • convection_coefficient — convective heat transfer coefficient $h$
  • resistance_fluid — fluid convection $R_f$
  • resistance_pipe — pipe wall conduction $R_p$
  • resistance_ULoop_borehole — borehole resistance $R_b$
  • resistance_ULoop_total_internal — total internal resistance $R_a$
  • resistance_ULoop_effective — effective resistance $R_b^*$
  • resistance_coaxial — coaxial resistances $R_1$, $R_{12}$
  • resistance_coaxial_effective — coaxial effective resistance $R_b^*$

Water property functions

  • water_k, water_cp, water_ρ, water_μ
  • fluid_property — water/MPG/MEG properties via CoolProp
